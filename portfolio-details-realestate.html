RealEstate.ae

Building a measurable lead generation system for a Dubai real estate brokerage.

I joined RealEstate.ae during an early growth stage, when the brokerage was still building its sales and marketing infrastructure. My role has focused on creating and improving the lead generation engine across Google Ads, Meta Ads, landing pages, conversion tracking, CRM feedback, and multi-market campaign execution. The work has combined hands-on paid acquisition with the technical and operational systems needed to understand what happens after a lead is generated.

The starting point

A growing brokerage needed a stronger acquisition and measurement setup

RealEstate.ae was scaling its project marketing while the wider marketing stack was still being developed. The immediate priority was to generate consistent demand while improving tracking quality, landing-page journeys, and the feedback loop between marketing and sales.

What I walked into

Paid acquisition was active but the wider measurement and optimisation framework was still evolving. GA4 and GTM needed refinement, project landing pages required stronger conversion tracking, sales feedback needed to feed campaign decisions more directly, and the business was expanding into new projects and international markets quickly.

Core priorities

  • Generate qualified buyer enquiries across Google and Meta
  • Improve conversion tracking across forms, calls, and WhatsApp
  • Strengthen project landing-page measurement and QA
  • Use CRM feedback to evaluate lead quality, not only platform CPL
  • Support rapid campaign launches across multiple projects and markets
  • Build a cleaner basis for reporting and optimisation decisions
The focus: build a dependable lead generation process where campaign performance, tracking accuracy, and downstream lead quality could be evaluated together.
My role

Hands-on performance marketing with technical ownership

My work covers campaign execution and the systems around it. I manage acquisition activity directly while also working on tracking, landing-page journeys, CRM feedback, creative direction, and coordination with sales, developers, and designers.

Performance ownership

  • Plan, launch, and optimise Google Search and Meta lead generation campaigns
  • Manage budgets, CPL, conversion rates, search terms, audiences, and creative performance
  • Structure campaigns around project, market, language, and buyer intent
  • Use CRM feedback to assess lead quality beyond platform-reported conversions

Cross-functional execution

  • Work with developers on GTM, data layer, forms, and tracking fixes
  • Coordinate with designers and video editors on paid creative production
  • Work with sales teams on lead quality, offer positioning, and market feedback
  • Support landing-page structure, messaging, and conversion improvements
What I improved

The acquisition system behind RealEstate.ae

The work has centred on making paid media easier to launch, measure, diagnose, and improve across a fast-moving real estate environment.

Google Search acquisition

Managed high-intent search campaigns across brand, project, and area-focused demand.

  • Structured keyword and match-type strategy around buyer intent
  • Reviewed search terms and built negative keyword coverage
  • Monitored CPC, CTR, conversion rate, CPL, and impression share
  • Aligned ads and landing pages more closely around project intent

Meta lead generation

Ran multi-market lead generation campaigns across project launches, languages, and audience segments.

  • Built campaigns across UAE and international buyer markets
  • Tested creative angles, messaging, formats, and lead forms
  • Monitored frequency, CPM, CTR, CPL, and lead quality
  • Reallocated budgets based on platform performance and CRM feedback

GTM + GA4 tracking

Improved the measurement layer so real conversions could be distinguished from incomplete interactions.

  • Worked with GTM data layer events across project and area landing pages
  • Validated generate_lead only after successful backend confirmation
  • Tracked calls, WhatsApp clicks, callback forms, and lead submissions
  • Used GTM preview and GA4 debugging to QA conversion behaviour

Landing-page optimisation

Supported project and area landing pages with a stronger focus on message match, conversion paths, and tracking reliability.

  • Reviewed CTA placement and form journeys
  • Helped align page messaging with paid search and social campaigns
  • QA tested hero CTAs, pop-ups, sticky forms, and bottom forms
  • Worked with developers to ensure successful submissions reached the CRM correctly

CRM lead-quality feedback

Brought downstream sales outcomes into performance discussions so optimisation was not based on CPL alone.

  • Reviewed lead quality and sales feedback by campaign and source
  • Compared raw CPL with qualification and buyer intent
  • Used CRM feedback to inform budget and targeting decisions
  • Worked toward stronger CRM-to-platform optimisation signals

International campaign execution

Supported market-specific planning across different buyer profiles, currencies, languages, and offers.

  • Adapted messaging for UAE and international audiences
  • Supported English, Arabic, Urdu, and French campaign variants
  • Adjusted pricing and offer communication by market
  • Coordinated localised creative and lead-form requirements

Tracking QA and technical troubleshooting

A significant part of the role has involved diagnosing issues between the ad platform, website, tracking layer, and CRM. I have worked through GTM trigger logic, data layer events, backend-confirmed form success, consent behaviour, CSP-related tracking limitations, and duplicate CRM-entry checks to make sure reported conversions represent genuine leads.

Impact

What this work changed

The strongest improvement has been a more connected view of acquisition performance, from the ad click through to the lead entering the CRM.

Cleaner conversion measurement

Lead events are validated more carefully, including backend-confirmed submissions and QA across different form journeys.

Stronger optimisation decisions

Campaign decisions increasingly combine platform metrics with CRM lead quality and sales feedback.

Faster multi-market execution

Campaigns can be launched and adapted across multiple projects, countries, languages, and creative requirements with a more repeatable process.

Most importantly: performance is evaluated as a connected acquisition journey, with paid media, landing pages, tracking, and CRM outcomes informing each other.
